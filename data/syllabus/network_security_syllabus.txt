The main security properties of a computer system will be introduced and discussed in detail. Approaches to improve security at the various levels of the network protocol stack will then be presented and analyzed. The course adopts an “offensive security” approach. Concepts related to the so-called vulnerability–threat–attack chain will be illustrated. Techniques for preparing a cyber attack, such as footprinting, scanning and enumeration, will be described. The final phase of an attack, known as exploitation, will then be presented. Topics such as firewalling, intrusion detection, malware analysis and protection against DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) attacks will be introduced. The main techniques of so-called ethical hacking will finally be presented and analyzed in detail.
